Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Meltaway processes, why, and what choices you have.

Data controller

Luca Campegiani (Worldlyke Studios).
Privacy email: worldlykehub@gmail.com.

In short

  • There is no account: we don't ask for your name, email or any other data that directly identifies you.
  • Your progress stays on your phone (and, if you turned it on, in your Google account backup).
  • Ads and usage statistics go through Google services; in the European Union, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time from Settings > Privacy options.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play: we never see or store payment details.

Data on your phone

The game saves a file with your progress on your phone: levels, in-game currencies, items, settings and the identifiers of purchases already received (so they are never credited twice). We don't send it to us. If Google backup is on, Android copies this file (and only this file) to the encrypted backup of your Google account, to restore it on a new phone.

Legal bases

  • Personalised ads, statistics and crash reports in the EU, UK and Switzerland: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads: legitimate interest in funding the free game.
  • Purchases: performance of the purchase contract.

Retention

  • Data on your phone: as long as the game is installed (and in your Google backup according to your account settings).
  • Usage statistics: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ads and purchases: according to Google's policies.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google services may process data in the United States and other countries, with the safeguards required by law (standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw consent: in the game, Settings > Privacy options.
  • Reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Delete your data: see Data deletion.
  • You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object and to data portability, and you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top; important changes are also announced in the game.
