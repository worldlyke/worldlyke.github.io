Data deletion

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page is about the Android game Meltaway, developed by Worldlyke Studios. It explains how to delete the data related to the game, which data is deleted, which is kept, and for how long.

How to request deletion

  1. Write to worldlykehub@gmail.com with the subject "Meltaway data deletion".
  2. Include your installation code: you find it in the game under Settings > Installation code (tap "Copy" and paste it into the email). Without an account it is the only way to find the statistics of your installation. If you made purchases, add the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in Google's email receipt).
  3. We confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days of the request.

The game has no account: we only ask for information that helps us find the data of your installation, never passwords or documents.

What you can delete yourself, right away

  • Progress on your phone: Android Settings > Apps > Meltaway > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Copy in your Google backup: Android Settings > Google > Backup (or the Google Drive app > Backups) > delete the device app backup.
  • Consent for ads and statistics: in the game, Settings > Privacy options, choose not to consent; from then on statistics and crash reports stop.
  • Advertising ID: Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.

Data deleted on request

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) linked to your installation.
  • The emails you sent us, after the request is closed.

Data kept

  • Purchases: order data is kept by Google Play, which handles it as the seller and for tax obligations; we can't delete it. The game keeps no payment details.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): they can't be linked to a person, so they aren't deleted on request; they are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Ads: data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its policies (policies.google.com/privacy); you can control it from your Google account.
  • Request log: for 12 months we keep only the date of the request and of its closure, to show it was handled.

Timing

  • Request confirmed: within 7 days.
  • Deletion completed: within 30 days.
  • If you don't ask, statistics are deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.
